Official Privacy Document

Privacy Policy for MoneyVault: Finance & FIRE

Last Updated: October 7, 2026 | Effective Date: October 7, 2026
Core Privacy Commitment: MoneyVault: Finance & FIRE is built on an offline-first architecture. Your personal financial transactions, account balances, and savings vault data are stored locally on your device. We do not require account registration, and we do not store or transmit your financial logs to our servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MoneyVault: Finance & FIRE ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through transparent data handling practices in accordance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy laws.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Because MoneyVault operates primarily offline on your device, we do not collect or store on our servers:

  • Your name, address, email address, or phone number (no account required).
  • Your bank account details, credit card credentials, or banking logins.
  • Your personal budgets, expenses, transactions, emergency fund targets, or investment portfolios.

3. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored strictly within your device's local storage (e.g., via SQLite / SharedPreferences):

  • Financial Vaults & Transactions: Bucket names, amounts, categories, dates, and simulated rates.
  • User Preferences: Preferred currency symbol (e.g., €, $, £), reminder notification times, and compact view settings.
  • Temporary Passes: Expiration timestamps for the temporary ad-rewarded Pro Pass.
  • Local Backups: Manual JSON exports generated solely when you choose to export your data.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Collected for App Functionality

To provide in-app subscriptions, premium feature verification, and optional rewarded video passes, the App integrates trusted third-party SDKs. These third parties may collect certain technical identifiers in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

A. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (RevenueCat & Google Play Billing)

We use RevenueCat and the Google Play In-App Billing API to process and verify purchases for MoneyVault Premium.

  • Data processed: Anonymous app user IDs, transaction receipts, active subscription status, purchase dates, and store entitlement IDs.
  • Purpose: To unlock premium features across sessions and restore previous purchases.
  • Privacy Policy: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

B. Advertising (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)

The App provides an optional rewarded video feature allowing users to unlock a temporary 2-minute Premium Pass by voluntarily viewing an ad. We use Google AdMob for this service.

  • Data processed: Device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), coarse location, device type, operating system version, and ad interaction diagnostics.
  • Purpose: To serve rewarded advertisements, prevent advertising fraud, and verify reward fulfillment.
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob Data Usage.

5. Device Permissions Requested

The App requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver optional, user-configured local reminders (e.g., daily expense check-in or weekly wealth review). Notifications are scheduled locally on the device.
  • INTERNET: Required only for communication with Google Play Billing, RevenueCat subscription verification, and Google AdMob rewarded ads.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all financial logs and vault data are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete all data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.
  • You can clear demo or custom transactions directly within the App's Data Center.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

8. Security

We value the security of your data. Local application data is protected by the standard security sandbox of the Android / iOS operating systems. We recommend protecting your device with a secure passcode or biometric lock.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.
